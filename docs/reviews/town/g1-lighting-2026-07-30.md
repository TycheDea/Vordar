# G1 lighting look gate — verdict

Date: 2026-07-30. Campaign Phase 1 close. Opus visual gate over 4 candidate
looks (L1-L4) plus the shipped-at-the-time amber dusk baseline, all rendered
through `zone_review`'s `--visuals-override` against the "start" zone's real
prop dressing and chapter03 interior.

## Verdict

**Adopt L3 (overcast) as the shipped default.** Runner-up: L2 (late
afternoon). The baseline (dusk) and L1/L4 are not adopted.

## Score table

| Look | Theme | TrueColor | A4-headroom | Depth | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 (blue hour) | 5 | 3 | 6 | 4 | 5 |
| L2 (late afternoon) | 4 | 9 | 4 | 8 | 3 |
| **L3 (overcast)** | **8** | **9** | **9** | **7** | **8** |
| L4 (moonlit) | 2 | 3 | 1 | 3 | 2 |
| baseline (dusk) | — | — | — | — | unusable |

## Key observations

- **L3** wins on true-color material read and VQ-A4 headroom by the widest
  margin of any candidate — a neutral grey key leaves materials reading
  their authored PBR color with no hue cast, and leaves the full VQ-A4
  chroma budget open for the votive-cool/candle-gold/threat-red role colors
  the palette reserves. It also scores highest on theme (semi-realistic dark
  fantasy "dread stillness" reads through flat overcast light) and mood,
  with depth close behind L2.
- **L2** ties L3 on TrueColor (9) and leads on Depth (8, directional late-day
  sun rakes the terrain), but scores weakest on Mood (3) and mid on Theme —
  a bright clear sky reads less "dark fantasy" than overcast gloom. Held as
  runner-up rather than adopted.
- **L1** and **L4** both score low across the board: L1 (blue-hour twilight)
  suffers a lilac value-collapse in interior shots (chapter03's nave loses
  tonal separation under the cool violet key); L4 (moonlit) is the weakest
  candidate, with blown highlights on lit surfaces against near-black crush
  everywhere else, and a stark interior-vs-exterior exposure mismatch (the
  night key that reads outdoors goes unusably dark inside the chapel).
- **Baseline** (the original amber dusk) was scored unusable by the gate:
  the warm cast dominates every material read, leaving no true-color
  headroom and no room for the candle-gold accent to read as distinct from
  the ambient field.

## Defects recorded

- **L4:** blown highlights + black crush + interior/exterior exposure
  inconsistency (see above).
- **L1:** lilac value-collapse in interior shots.
- **Horizon/ground-extent seam** visible in high-contrast interior door
  views — a VQ-A5 clause-9 (feel-checklist item 9, horizon fog) class of
  defect, shared across every look including baseline. Not look-specific;
  not fixed by this gate.
- **Wide-shot fog density too high at high camera altitude** — present in
  all four looks and the baseline. Shared defect, not a candidate
  differentiator. Carried forward as a **Phase 4 watch item**, not fixed
  now.

## Follow-ups

- **L2 re-check:** runner-up gets a two-frame re-check at the Phase 4 ship
  gate (before any final look lock), given how close it ran to L3 on
  TrueColor/Depth.
- **Wide-shot fog-at-altitude:** Phase 4 watch item (see Defects above).
- **Horizon/ground-extent seam:** VQ-A5 clause-9 class defect, unresolved,
  cross-look.
